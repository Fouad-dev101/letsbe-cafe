01 — Our Philosophy

Food that
remembers
where it came from.

Let's Be began with a simple question: what would it feel like to eat food that gives more than it takes? Everything we serve starts with whole ingredients, bought fresh from the market, prepared slowly and without shortcuts.

No refined sugar. No industrial oils. No compromises dressed up as convenience. Just vegetables, grains, fruit, nuts, seeds, herbs and spices — combined with intention and served in a room that feels like a long exhale.

Whether you've just come out of the water, are passing through Tamraght on the coastal road, or live here year-round, the table is set the same way: generous, colourful, and made to be lingered over.

"Healing isn't a trend. It's the oldest form of hospitality there is."

02 — Signature Experiences

Six reasons
to stay
a little longer.

Everything on our menu is built around a single idea — that food should leave you lighter than it found you. These are the plates, bowls and glasses people come back for.

01

Smoothie Bowls

Thick, cold-blended fruit and superfoods, finished with granola, seeds and whatever the market gave us that morning.

02

Healing Plates

Bowls built from wholegrains, roasted vegetables, legumes and a dressing that ties the whole thing together. The heart of the menu.

03

Cold-Pressed

Juices pressed to order. Nothing added, nothing heated, nothing wasted.

04

Specialty Coffee

Espresso, flat whites and filter — plus golden and matcha lattes for the days that call for something slower.

05

Raw Desserts

Cacao, dates, tahini and coconut. Sweetened by fruit, never by sugar.

06

Slow Breakfast

Served from 09:00. Sourdough, avocado, dukkah, fresh fruit — the kind of morning you don't rush.
